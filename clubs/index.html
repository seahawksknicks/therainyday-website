A VIP room called “The Schuylkill Express Lane” — no one moves, but everyone is honking.

Rocky Karaoke

Secret menu: the Broad Street Biscotti, served in a tiny traffic cone.

Motown & Order: SUV

Hidden inside a self-driving concept car that only makes left turns, this Motor City hotspot opens whenever someone says, “Actually, techno started here.”

This place has everything
1

Assembly-line bottle service where tiny robots install sparklers onto Faygo at terrifying speed.

2

A coat check run by retired goalie masks that judge your jacket and your childhood.

3

The Henry Ford Escape Room — you have 60 minutes to unionize a charcuterie board.

Newest party craze

Parallel Parkour

It’s that thing where two guys named Big Mike vault over orange barrels while a techno DJ samples potholes and a coney dog whispers municipal policy.

Secret menu: the Belle Isle Boilermaker, garnished with a tiny traffic report.

Peach, Please

In the basement of a brunch place that only serves reservations, this Atlanta fever dream is guarded by a valet who can smell if you used the correct airport terminal.

This place has everything
1

A trap choir in matching linen harmonizing over GPS instructions that keep rerouting through Buckhead.

2

Influencer confessional booths where you apologize to your ring light and receive a tiny lemon pepper wing.

3

A rooftop called “The Connector” — beautiful view, nobody gets home, everyone becomes a podcast guest.

Secret menu: the Hartsfield-Jackson Hot Pocket, available after a four-hour layover.
